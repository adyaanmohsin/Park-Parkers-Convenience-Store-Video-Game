Park & Parkers Convenience

It is January 1, 1999. You are Siwan Park, Korean-Canadian owner and the only clerk. Ring up customers properly at the counter — scan each item, enter the total, take cash, give change, and hand over a receipt. Spend earnings on shop upgrades so the corner store grows from bare 1999 stock to a lived-in neighbourhood fixture by 2016.
